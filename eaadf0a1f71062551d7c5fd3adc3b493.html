Vertical & crosswise

20 questions. Start small, build your carrying skills, then stretch to three and four digits.

Method reminder

Work from the rightmost diagonal to the left. Add all products on each diagonal, then add the incoming carry. Write the ones digit and carry the rest to the next diagonal. For the final diagonal, write the full remaining total.

For three-digit × three-digit questions, the diagonals contain 1, 2, 3, 2, 1 products. For four-digit × four-digit questions: 1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, 1.
